CaptiOCR

Free, open-source live caption transcriber for Windows and macOS.

CaptiOCR turns the live captions shown on your screen by Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Webex and other apps into a text transcript. It reads the caption area in real time with Tesseract OCR, entirely on your own computer: no cloud processing, no meeting bot, no audio recording and no account.

See the download page for system requirements and installation steps.

Features

  • 100% local processing: screenshots, OCR and transcripts never leave your device.
  • Works with any app that shows captions on screen: Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Webex, YouTube and more.
  • Duplicate filtering that keeps only new caption text in a clean .txt transcript.
  • OCR languages: English, Italian, French, German and Portuguese.
  • Free and open source under the MIT license, with no subscription or usage limits.

How it works

  1. Turn on live captions in your meeting or video app.
  2. Select the caption area on screen in CaptiOCR.
  3. CaptiOCR reads the captions as they appear and saves a local transcript you can summarize, search or share.
